## 👋 About Me

Data Analyst with 3 years of experience in telecom analytics, building dashboards and surfacing KPIs that commercial, product, and operations teams actually use. At Reliance and Airtel, I worked across the full analytics cycle — querying subscriber and billing data in SQL and Snowflake, cleaning and transforming raw datasets in Python and Pandas, and delivering Tableau and Power BI dashboards that tracked ARPU, churn, daily active users, and revenue per circle.

I've modeled dimensional schemas to standardize reporting across regional business units, designed KPI frameworks in Excel, run A/B tests on promotional pricing, and documented data definitions to reduce recurring analyst overhead. Currently completing my MS at MIT (May 2026) while continuing to sharpen skills in dbt, Looker, and statistical analysis.
